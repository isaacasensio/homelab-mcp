homelab-mcp

A personal, read-only MCP server for Gmail and Google Calendar.

Purpose

homelab-mcp lets its owner ask their AI assistant questions about their own email and calendar, such as "what's in my inbox today?" or "when is my next meeting?". It connects the assistant to Gmail and Google Calendar through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard for giving AI assistants access to tools and data.

It runs on a private home server and is used by a single person: its author. It is not offered as a service to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with Google once and grants read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar.
  2. When the owner asks their assistant a question, the assistant calls homelab-mcp.
  3. homelab-mcp fetches only the messages or events needed from Google and returns them to the assistant.

What it can do

  • List and search messages in the owner's Gmail inbox, and read a message.
  • List upcoming events in the owner's Google Calendar, and read an event.

Access is read-only. It cannot send, delete, or change email or calendar events.

Google permissions requested

  • gmail.readonly: read email messages and settings.
  • calendar.readonly: see and download calendars.
